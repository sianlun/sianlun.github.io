Cloud-based Radiation Dosimetry (TED2)

Cloud-based Intelligent Measurement, Monitoring and Analytics System in Support of Radiation Medicine

Period
Jul 2021 – Dec 2022
Funder
MOSTI (TED2)
Grant
RM 2,435,600
Role
Project Leader / PI
Reference
TEF05211044

Overview

This project develops an optical fiber scintillator-based electronic system for real-time medical radiation measurement (dosimetry), paired with a cloud-based intelligent data acquisition, monitoring, and analytics platform. The system addresses limitations of established dosimeters (MOSFET/diode/ion chamber) by offering safer, real-time measurement capabilities at distances from the radiation source, covering a wide sensitivity range from diagnostic imaging to therapeutic levels.

The cloud-based component enables a national radiation monitoring network, providing dose records for patients and supporting remote quality assurance in radiotherapy and diagnostic imaging. The project targets commercialization through partner Alypz Sdn Bhd, serving Malaysia's 58 external beam radiotherapy facilities, 530 general x-ray units, and 642 specialized diagnostic facilities.
