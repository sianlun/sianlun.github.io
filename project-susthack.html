SustHack

Synchronizing Sustainable Development Actions between Finland and Malaysia: The Hackathon Approach

Period
2019 – 2021
Funder
EDUFI (Asia Programme 2019)
Grant
~€32,000
Role
Partner / Co-Investigator

Overview

SustHack aims to emphasize sustainability perception in computing education by utilizing co-creative hackathons addressing local sustainability challenges in both Finland and Malaysia. These cross-national hackathons emphasize the possibilities of IT in sustainable development and increase the understanding of sustainability in the computing field.

The project organized 6 hackathons (4 physical with staff/student mobility and 2 virtual) during 2019–2021, engaging PhD and master's students as well as interested citizens and local stakeholders. Activities focused on staff/student mobility, common study modules, and curricula development reflecting lessons learned from the hackathons. SustHack served as a trailblazer in leveraging hackathons for sustainability education and laid the groundwork for subsequent projects like ImpactXChange.

Partners
